Rental Yield

The Rental Yield is a measure of the return on a property investment, calculated as:

Rental Yield = (Average Rent Price × 12) ÷ Average Sale Price × 100%

The Rental Yield is a real estate metric that compares the rental income generated by a property to its fair market value, expressed as a percentage.

All Risk Yield

The All Risk Yield adjusts the Rental Yield by accounting for operational costs, and is calculated as:

Ko :The All Risks Yield, which accounts for costs and expenses and rental growth over a specified investment period.Here we evaluate the investment over a five-year period

e :The Equated Yield, which is essentially the internal rate of return (IRR) for the property's cash flows.It includes the initial investment, the net profit after deducting costs and expenses, and the residual value (i.e., the property's value at the end of the investment period). It also takes into account the rental income growth rate.

t :the period between each rent review.

g :The annual rental income growth rate.

Here, the operational costs are estimated at 34% of the gross annual rental income. This percentage is based on data from the Ireland Rental Income Statisticsby Revenue Irish Tax and Customs, which tracks the average costs for property management, maintenance, and taxation for rental properties in Ireland. In Ireland the rent can be renewed every twelve months, so I have set Rent Review Period to one year.Based on Ireland's highest rental income growth rate and the average inflation rate, I have set the growth rate at 2%.
